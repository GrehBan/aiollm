An `assistant` `Turn` can carry `native`, and `to_turn()` of a response that called built-in tools, or paused (`IncompleteReason.PAUSED`), returns one: the provider needs its own record of the turn, such as Anthropic's encrypted search results, to go on with it. A paused response without text has a turn too, and a conversation may now end with an `assistant` turn that has `native`, to continue the pause; any other `assistant` turn still cannot end it, and an ordinary text answer still gives a plain turn. A provider adapter sends `Turn.native` when it is its own, as it does for a `ToolCallTurn`, and one that does not ignores it, so a provider package on this core changes nothing until it takes these.
